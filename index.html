My approach

Operational leadership is not about having every answer. It is about knowing how to find the right one quickly.

See the bottleneck

Monitor performance, understand dependencies and identify risks before they become larger problems.

02

Execute

Move from analysis to action and keep the operation moving toward the required outcome.
